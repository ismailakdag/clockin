# Clockin 1.1.6

- Every button, menu and field now looks like something you can click: buttons have a surface, the pointer turns into a hand, and they react when you press them.
- Pickers are full-size fields that open a menu with a checkmark on your choice. Ranges like 7D / 30D / 3M / All are segmented controls you can also move through with the arrow keys.
- Themes are chosen from preview cards instead of a list of names, and Settings is grouped into clear sections.
- Dates and times in past entries and the rate schedule open a proper calendar or clock.
- The menu bar icon and the window header now show the Clockin companion: asleep when you are not clocked in, awake while you work, with pause bars when paused.
- Text is larger and calmer throughout, and decorative emoji and stock phrases are gone.
- Fixed: the menu bar panel cut off "Clock out" at larger interface sizes, the pinned timer did not grow with the interface size, and light theme text was hard to read on cards.
