# Clockin mission collection

Clockin connects real work with an earned space collection. Show the next concrete
milestone, the reward silhouette and progress; keep explanatory text behind a tap.

Six ranks: Launch (mint), Orbit (blue), Lunar (violet), Solar (amber), Galactic
(rose), Eternal (pale gold). The final tier contains only Time titan, Year of
hundreds, Year-round and Permanent practice. Existing earning conditions and IDs
stay intact; rank assignment does not award XP or coins.

The insignia use authored 100-unit vector geometry: launch vehicle, satellite
orbit, lunar transit, solar core, three-arm galaxy, and a faceted singularity.
No emoji or SF Symbol is used as a tier logo or mission seal. Motion echoes work:
flight trails, streak signals, calendar orbits, scanning logs, habitat nodes and
helmet arcs. A small version appears on the tier tab; the same visual language
carries into the selected tier, earned tiles and tappable reward preview.
Animations are finite, user-triggered, and skip Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode.

Six tier tabs replace the long tier stack. Large accessibility text uses a 3×2
selector. Each page shows one tier, one next mission and its badges. Avoid nested
scroll views. General text uses the app's typography and theme; the reward preview
has a dark space field so every theme retains legible, luminous insignia.

## Free room gifts

Atatürk portrait and Turkish flag are free catalog entries, automatically owned
on refresh for both existing and new users. They occupy different wall slots and
can be used together. Equipping is optional. Both retain their orientation when
the room is mirrored. Free gifts never count as purchases.

The photograph is bundled unchanged from
[Wikimedia Commons — Atatürk Kemal](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atat%C3%BCrk_Kemal.jpg),
listed there as public domain in Turkey; original photographer unknown. Downloaded
2026-09-21. The app fits the full image in a small room frame at runtime.

The flag is code-native vector geometry following the proportions shown in
[Wikimedia Commons — Flag of Turkey](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Turkey.svg),
a public-domain geometric flag reference. Red is #E30A17. No remote image fetch
occurs while using Clockin.
