# Clockin 1.1.2

- Click the menu bar icon to open a panel with your timer, earnings and today's progress, and clock in, pause or clock out from there. The panel opens over full-screen apps, so you stay where you are instead of being taken to the desktop.
- A bolder menu bar icon shows at a glance whether you are working: an open ring when you are not clocked in, a solid stopwatch while a session runs, and pause bars when paused.
- In minimal menu bar mode, nothing appears beside the icon until you clock in; then the timer starts counting. It no longer shows "0m · $0" while you are not working.
- The "T" before the timer is gone.
- Cancelling a session from the panel asks you to confirm first.
- Right-click the menu bar icon for Open Clockin, Check for Updates and Quit.
