# Clockin 1.1.4

- The focus companion moves like the one on the Clockin website. It blinks, dips its antenna, glows, types, sips coffee and hops, in a random order, so it never repeats a fixed loop.
- It says hello when you are not clocked in, types while you work, has a coffee on a break and celebrates if you choose Victory. Click it to make it hop and celebrate.
- Standing poses sway gently, and the companion leans toward your pointer.
- The animation is smoother and uses almost no CPU, and it stops while its window is hidden.
